Was I overcharged?

Use the distance and waiting above.

—

Under the Road Transport Act 2018 (s. 35(3) & s. 81), a driver must charge by the meter and cannot demand more. Overcharging can mean a fine of up to Tk 50,000 and/or up to 6 months’ jail. Complain to BRTA (brta.gov.bd). Note: BRTA withdrew its Feb 2025 enforcement drive order on 16 Feb 2025 — the fare itself is unchanged.
